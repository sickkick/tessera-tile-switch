# Changelog

## 0.1.2 - 2026-10-11
- Offered on a 4 MB screen that is already at Tessera's flash limit. The tile only draws with what the screen already has.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-10-11
- Fits a small 4 MB screen. It adds about 1 KB, because it draws with the screen's own fonts and colours.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-11
- A tile with the on/off switch on the right, for a light, switch, fan and the other things that turn on and off.
