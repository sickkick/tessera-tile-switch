# Changelog

## 0.1.4 - 2026-10-11
- The switch sits along the bottom of the tile, with the knob in the bottom right while it is on.
- A tile two columns wide uses the larger title. A one-cell tile keeps the small one.
- The inspector has an Icon choice. Automatic follows the entity.

## 0.1.3 - 2026-10-11
- The switch on a one-cell tile is a short pill, so the name keeps its room.

## 0.1.2 - 2026-10-11
- Offered on a 4 MB screen that is already at Tessera's flash limit. The tile only draws with what the screen already has.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-10-11
- Fits a small 4 MB screen. It adds about 1 KB, because it draws with the screen's own fonts and colours.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-11
- A tile with the on/off switch on the right, for a light, switch, fan and the other things that turn on and off.
